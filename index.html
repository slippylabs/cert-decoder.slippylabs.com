TLS CERTIFICATE DECODER

Paste a PEM certificate, a full chain, or drop a .pem/.crt/.cer/.der file. The ASN.1 is parsed here in the page — a private key or an internal hostname never leaves the machine.

Decoding only — this reads what the certificate says about itself. It does not verify the signature against the issuer's key or check revocation, so a self-signed certificate claiming to be a well-known CA will still be shown as it is written.
